Dear Sir or Madam,

I, \myFirstName {} \myLastName, am the self-petitioner of a pending Form I-140 Immigrant Petition for Alien Worker (receipt number \myEBcase), filed under the EB-2 National Interest Waiver (NIW) classification pursuant to INA §203(b)(2)(B).I am submitting this statement to confirm my intent to continue working in the occupational field specified in my Form I-140 petition as part of my Form I-485 Application to Adjust Status.

As outlined in my Form I-140 petition, my proposed endeavor is to continue my research and work in the field of electric power engineering, specifically focusing on simulating, modeling, and optimizing grid integration dynamics to facilitate the transition toward more renewable energy sources. This endeavor involves developing advanced control schemes for distributed energy resources and battery energy storage systems, testing and validating these schemes in both offline and real-time simulations, and disseminating the results through peer-reviewed publications, conference presentations, workshops, seminars, and open-source software contributions.

I am fully committed to advancing this proposed endeavor, which has substantial merit and national importance, as demonstrated in my I-140 petition. To illustrate my commitment and capacity to pursue this endeavor, I was employed as an Energy Technology Researcher II at the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory from August 2018 until my layoff at the end of September 2025 due to insufficient funding to maintain my position. In this role, I actively contributed to research projects that aligned with my proposed endeavor, including the development and validation of advanced control schemes for distributed energy resources and battery energy storage systems. Following my layoff, I am actively searching for and intend to secure new employment in the same occupational field of electric power engineering, with a focus on grid integration dynamics and renewable energy transition. I have applied to multiple positions at organizations including Argonne National Laboratory, California Independent System Operator, Midcontinent Independent System Operator, ISO New England, Pacific Gas and Electric Company, Tesla Energy, and Heron Power, and I am prepared to continue advancing my proposed endeavor through any suitable role, self-employment, or consulting opportunities in this field.

While my proposed endeavor is not tied to a specific position or employer, my prior employment at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory and ongoing job search confirm my dedication to working in electric power engineering, focusing on the simulation, modeling, and optimization of grid integration dynamics to support the transition to renewable energy sources, as described in my Form I-140 petition.

I affirm that I intend to continue working in this field upon approval of my adjustment of status.

I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States that the foregoing is true and correct.
