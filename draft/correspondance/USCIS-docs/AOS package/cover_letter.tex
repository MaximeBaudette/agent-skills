\letterSubject{Concurrent Filing of Forms I-485, I-765, and I-131}

Dear Immigration Officer:

I am respectfully submitting this letter following the previous rejection of my initial filing package due to incomplete application forms.
I apologize for the error and trust that after a proof-reading of all submitted materials, everything is now complete. 

This letter is in support of the adjustment of status of myself to that of lawful permanent residents under section 245 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, 8 U.S.C. § 1255. I, \myFirstName {} \myLastName, am the self-petitioner of a pending I-140 petition under National Interest Waiver (NIW), pursuant to section 203(b)(2) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, 8 U.S.C. 1153(b)(2). Specifically, this letter will show that I have met all the requirements under INA 8 USC § 1255 and therefore are eligible to file this application to adjust our immigration status to that of lawful permanent residents.

\subsubsection*{A. I am Currently in and Have Continuously Maintained Lawful Non-Immigrant Status Since My Last Entry to the United States}

I am currently in H-1B non-immigrant status. My H-1B non-immigrant status will expire on November 29, 2025 at the end of the grace period following my layoff on September 30, 2025. I last entered the United States on this H-1B visa on May 18, 2025. I have maintained lawful non-immigrant visa status since my last entry to the U.S. on May 18, 2025.

\subsubsection*{B. Visa Numbers Immediately Available at the Time of Filing}

My pending I-140 petition is under the preference of National Interest Waiver pursuant to Section 203(b)(2) of the Immigration and Nationality Act.  In addition, I was born in France. According to the latest USCIS Adjustment of Status Filing Chart, my priority date is current. According to this month’s USCIS Adjustment of Status Filing Chart, I am therefore eligible to file these adjustment of status applications.

\subsubsection*{C. I Self-Petitioned My Pending I-140 NIW Petition - Therefore, I-485 Supplement J Is Not Required}

I self-petitioned my pending I-140 NIW petition, and thus I am not required to have a valid job offer, nor does I have a petitioning (Form I-140) employer. As indicated in the I-485 Instructions and Supplement J instructions,``…Because these employment-based immigrant visa categories are not tied to a specific job offer, individuals seeking or granted classification as an alien of extraordinary ability, or seeking or granted a National Interest Waiver of the job offer requirement, do not have to file Supplement J when filing Form I-485 or to request job portability under INA section 204(j)'' Therefore, I-485 Supplement J, Confirmation of Bona Fide Job Offer or Request for Job Portability Under INA Section 204(j), is not required for my I-485 application, which is filed based on an approved  EB-2 National Interest Waiver (NIW) petition.

\subsubsection*{D. I am a French Citizen of Good Standing and Will Not Become Public Charges to the United States}

I am a French citizen of good standing. 
My admission as permanent resident will not constitute a social burden to the United States, given my employment history at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, where I worked from August 2018 to September 2025, where my latest annual salary was $\$129,264.00$. I am actively seeking new employment, upon my layoff, I received severance pay in the amount of $\$11,562.18$ and have sufficient savings to meet all my financial obligations and needs until I find new employment.

This letter is also in support of my concurrent filing of Form I-765 (Application for Employment Authorization), and Form I-131 (Application for Travel Document). This submission includes all required forms, fees, and supporting documents.

With these facts in mind, it is respectfully requested that you consider these applications and the evidence submitted herewith, and upon consideration, approve the applications and request.
