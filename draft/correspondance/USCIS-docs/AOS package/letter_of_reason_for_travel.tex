\letterSubject{Reason for Travel Statement for Form I-131 Application for Advance Parole}

Dear Sir or Madam,

I, \myFirstName {} \myLastName, am the self-petitioner of a pending Form I-140 Immigrant Petition for Alien Worker (receipt number \myEBcase), filed under the EB-2 National Interest Waiver (NIW) classification. I am submitting this statement to support my Form I-131 Application for Advance Parole, filed concurrently with my Form I-485 Application to Adjust Status, to explain my reasons for requesting permission to travel abroad while my adjustment of status is pending.

My need to travel is driven by strong family ties in France, where my immediate family resides. I have a well-established history of visiting them multiple times yearly for occasions like birthdays and Christmas to maintain close familial bonds. Additionally, my grandmother, who turned 93 in August 2025, is experiencing declining health. Given her advanced age, any health issue could rapidly deteriorate, necessitating my ability to travel to France on short notice within the next 12–24 months.

These planned and potential emergency trips are temporary, typically lasting no more than a few weeks, and do not indicate any intent to abandon my permanent residence application. I am fully committed to returning to the United States after each trip to continue pursuing my professional endeavors in electric power engineering, as outlined in my I-140 petition, and to maintain my adjustment of status process.

I respectfully request Advance Parole to facilitate these necessary travels without jeopardizing my pending Form I-485 application.

I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States that the foregoing is true and correct.
