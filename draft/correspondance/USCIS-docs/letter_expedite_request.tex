Dear USCIS Officer,

I respectfully request expedited adjudication of my Form I-765 Application for Employment Authorization (receipt number \myEADcase) based on severe financial loss to myself as an individual, in accordance with USCIS expedite criteria outlined in the USCIS Policy Manual, Volume 1, Part A, Chapter 5.

On October 1, 2025, I was laid off from my position as Energy Technology Researcher II at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. I filed my Form I-485 Adjustment of Status package, including the concurrent Form I-765 and Form I-131, in mid-October 2025. At the time of filing, USCIS publicly reported processing times for Form I-765 (based on pending I-485) of several months, which led me to believe standard processing would allow me to resume employment before my financial situation became critical.

Since my layoff over five months ago, I have had no income. As I am not currently authorized to work in the United States, I have been unable to secure new employment despite active and ongoing job searches in my field of renewable energy grid integration research. Prospective employers have either not responded to my applications upon learning that sponsorship would be required to start immediately, or have indicated during initial discussions that they would welcome re-contact once I receive my Employment Authorization Document.

I am now entering my sixth month of unemployment and continue to face essential recurring monthly expenses, including rent, car loan payments, insurance premiums (including health insurance), utilities, and groceries. My personal savings have been steadily depleting to cover these obligations and will soon be insufficient to sustain my basic living needs. This situation has created genuine and severe financial hardship that was unforeseen at the time of filing.

I have attached a copy of my layoff notice as supporting documentation. I am happy to provide additional evidence—such as recent bank statements showing the depletion of savings, correspondence from prospective employers confirming the need for employment authorization, or proof of ongoing expenses—upon request.

I respectfully ask that USCIS exercise its discretion to expedite adjudication of my Form I-765. This would enable me to return promptly to productive work in a field that supports U.S. national interests in renewable energy transition and grid optimization, while alleviating the severe financial distress I am experiencing.
Thank you for your time and compassionate consideration of this request. I am available at the contact information provided above to furnish any further information or documentation.
