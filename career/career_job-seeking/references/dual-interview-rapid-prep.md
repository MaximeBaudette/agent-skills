# Dual-Interview Rapid Prep — Back-to-Back Stakeholder Pattern

## When This Applies
Maxime has multiple interviews scheduled **same day, back-to-back** with different stakeholders (e.g. peer technical + hiring decision-maker). Each conversation serves a different purpose and needs distinct prep.

## Pattern Recognition
Signal: "I have an interview with [Person A, technical] at 12:30 and [Person B, decision-maker] at 13:30"

## Stakeholder Typology & Prep Focus

| Stakeholder Type | What They Assess | Prep Focus | Example Question from Them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Peer / Senior IC** (e.g. Craig Breaden) | Technical depth, collaboration, methodology | Deep technical alignment. Show you've thought about the same problems. Ask about architecture choices (sim platform, test framework, lab layout). | "How would you design the HIL validation pipeline?" |
| **Hiring Manager / Head of** (e.g. Ed McCullough) | Judgment, autonomy, function-building, startup pace | Vision & execution. Position yourself as someone who builds from scratch. Ask about success criteria in year 1, team growth plans, biggest risks. | "What does success look like for this function in year 1?" |

## Prep Execution (Sub-2 Hour Window)

### First 30 min: Stakeholder Intelligence
1. Who are they? (role, background, what they care about)
2. What's their relationship to the role? (peer? boss? skip-level?)
3. What do they need to validate about you?

### Next 20 min: Frame Per Stakeholder
- **Peer/Technical**: "You already have the methodology, now show depth." Emphasize shared technical language, architecture choices, lessons learned.
- **Decision-Maker**: "You can build what they need from scratch." Emphasize autonomy, execution bias, function-building.

### Last 10 min: Prep In-Between
The gap between interviews is usually 0-15 min. Prep in advance:
- First interview ends → have 2-3 min to reset
- Don't try to review docs between them — you'll switch contexts too fast
- Prep a one-sentence transition: "For the next conversation, I'm the person who builds validation functions, not just runs tests"

## Conversation Hooks Per Stakeholder

### To the Technical Peer
- "What simulation platform are you leaning toward?"
- "How are you thinking about the split between SIL, HIL, and PHIL?"
- "What's the lab timeline — when do you need first validation capability?"

### To the Decision-Maker
- "What does success look like for the [validation/controls/test] function in year 1?"
- "How does this team interact with [related team] day-to-day?"
- "What's the biggest risk in the rollout that validation needs to catch?"

## Pitfalls

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Using the same pitch for both interviews | Adjust emphasis: peer = technical depth, HM = vision & function-building |
| Taking too long in interview 1 → late for interview 2 | Watch the clock. Know your hard stop. |
| Trying to review full prep docs between slots | Don't. Prep one page per stakeholder, review it before the day starts. |
| Mentioning comp questions to the technical interviewer | Redirect: "I've discussed expectations with [recruiter name]." |
| Not having water ready for back-to-back | Keep a glass nearby between slots. |
