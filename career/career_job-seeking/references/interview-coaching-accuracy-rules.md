# Interview Coaching Accuracy Rules & Best Practices

## Core Principle
ALL claims about Maxime's projects/experience in interview prep materials must be factually accurate. Research before writing. Never embellish TRL, deployment status, or scope.

## Role Positioning Strategy
For validation engineering roles (like Intersect Power):
- Emphasize "build-and-validate mindset" 
- Frame experience as "taking concepts and making them work"
- Connect to "getting it right before field deployment"
- Highlight testing and validation background

## Interview Material Creation Process
1. **Research first** - Verify actual project status, deployment scope, TRL
2. **Be conservative** - Under-promise, over-deliver on facts
3. **Use precise language** - "Lab-demonstrated" vs "production", "reference implementation" vs "deployed"
4. **Cross-check sources** - CV, project documentation, published papers
5. **Update continuously** - Refine as new information emerges

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- ❌ "Production" when actually "research/prototype/lab"
- ❌ "Deployed at utilities" when actually "reference implementation"
- ❌ "Managing thousands of DERs" when actually "simulation/validation"
- ❌ Embellishing TRL levels beyond actual status
