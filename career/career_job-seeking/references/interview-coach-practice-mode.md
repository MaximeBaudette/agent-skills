# Interview Coach — Practice Mode (Prep Exists)

When prep docs already exist at `career/leads/<YYYY-MM>-<company>-<role>/`, skip Phases 2-6 and enter **Conversational Practice Mode** instead.

## Flow

### 1. Load context
- Load the existing prep files from the lead directory (`interview-prep.md`, `role-analysis.md`, `pitch-coaching.md`)
- Load the lead's `job_leads.json` entry
- Load Maxime's PROFILE and CV for reference

### 2. Pitch rehearsal
- If a `pitch-coaching.md` exists, use its Keep/Change/Revised format:
  - **✅ What to Keep** — what worked in their delivery
  - **❌ What to Change** — specific fixes (3 sections max to avoid overload)
  - **✏️ Revised Pitch** — blockquote of the corrected version
- Ask Maxime to deliver the 2-min "Tell me about yourself" as if live
- Coach on: clarity, timing (target 90-120s), structure (past→present→why this role), energy
- Don't let him read — must be conversational
- If STT is broken, ask Maxime to type it

### 3. Q&A simulation
- Fire questions from the prep doc's question bank, **one at a time**
- After each answer: give brief specific feedback (what worked + 1 sharpening point)
- Push for full STAR+R answers, not one-liners
- Coach on: bridging to strengths, concrete tech details, honest framing
- Flag when answers drift into rambling, victim framing, or jargon for non-technical interviewers

### 4. Weakness pressure-test
- Run the hardest weakness scenarios from the prep doc's analysis
- For Intersect: PhD gap, research-to-industry, ERCOT blind spot, no validation engineer title, C++ adjacency
- Each round: hit them with the toughest version of the question, then debrief

### 5. Smart questions practice
- Have Maxime practice asking his 2-3 selected questions out loud
- Target: sound natural and curious, not rehearsed
- Coach on tone, framing, and follow-up

### 6. Wrap
- Ask: "What do you want to drill more?"
- End with logistics check: Zoom link, timing, contact, camera/lighting/sound
- Confirm pre-interview checklist completion

## Key Rules
- Never re-run research or re-write the doc unless Maxime explicitly asks
- Keep the energy high — this is game-day prep, not classroom teaching
- Give one specific praise item per answer before critique
- When Maxime answers a question well, say so. Specific praise > generic.
- Questions one at a time: fire one, wait for answer, debrief, move to next
- If Maxime corrects a factual claim in the prep docs, stop and fix it immediately across ALL lead doc files
