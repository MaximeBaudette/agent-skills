# Homepage Design Audit — Engineered By Breaden Comparison

Reference: `engineeredbybreaden.com` (Craig Breaden) benchmarked against `baudette.fr` (Maxime Baudette).
Date: 2026-05-26

## Key Difference

Craig's site **tells you who he is**. Maxime's site **tells you what his job is**. The gap is human connection, not technical detail.

## What Craig Does Well

### 1. Distinctive Theme (Terminal/CLI)
- Opens with `$ _` and structures sections as `$ ls -la ./posts/`, `$ cat topics.txt`
- Gimmick fits his identity (controls engineer, systems thinker)
- **Apply to Maxime:** Power-systems theme — one-line diagram, phasor motif, SCIENCE aesthetic

### 2. Personal Photo with Character
- Photo with his dog → instantly humanizing, memorable
- Maxime uses a standard professional headshot — correct but forgettable

### 3. Emoji Section Headers
- `# about`, `# skills`, `# interests`, `# life.log`, `# reading`, `# musings`
- Each with an emoji (🏴‍☠️ 🛠️ 🐇 🎯 📚 ✍️)
- Creates instant visual hierarchy and scannability

### 4. Skills in Interactive Categories
- Collapsible groups: Power Systems & Control, Industrial Control & Telemetry, Software Development, Real-Time Simulation, Platform Architecture
- Each with a tagline + domain badges with emojis
- Much richer than a flat list

### 5. "Interests" Section (Hover Animation)
- AI & Optimization, Quantum Computing, Control Theory, Home Automation, Power Electronics, Energy Markets, Philosophy, CS, DeFi
- Shows intellectual curiosity beyond job description
- Descriptions appear on hover → engaging

### 6. "life.log" — Hobbies Section
- Running, Cycling, Chess, Dog Dad with enthusiasm levels
- Maximum humanization. Makes you want to have coffee with him.

### 7. Currently Reading
- 3 books with genres + Goodreads link
- Shows he's curious and well-read

### 8. Clean, Single-Scroll Design
- Everything fits in one scroll, concise, no clutter

## Design Philosophy

> **Tell who you are, not just what you do.**

Craig's site works because every section reinforces *personality*:
- About → location pins, dog, chess with nephew
- Skills → practical context, not keyword dump
- Interests → rabbit holes he falls into
- Life.log → enthusiasm levels, not dry bullet points

Maxime's site currently reads like a CV rendered as a web page. The fix is adding **texture** — opinion, enthusiasm, personal detail. Academic professionalism is table stakes; personality is the differentiator.
